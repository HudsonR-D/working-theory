Ten mix-ups civilians actually make.

Each card teaches a name and why the name exists. None of them is a test you can give another person.

Everyday worry vs. an anxiety disorder

Occasional anxiety is part of life. An anxiety-disorder name is for fear or worry that stays, spreads, and gets in the way.

Sadness vs. depression

Sadness answers a loss. Depression is a weeks-long cluster that changes living.

Personality style vs. personality disorder

The word disorder is doing work. Most difficult people never meet it.

Trauma response vs. “they’re choosing this”

After threat, a body can keep scanning. That is not a free pass.

ADHD / executive function vs. disrespect

Time blindness can look like not caring. Not caring is also a real option.

Autistic social battery vs. rudeness

The name exists so “doesn’t do small talk” is not automatically contempt.

Narcissistic pattern vs. the internet’s “narcissist”

Most selfish evenings never reach the clinical name.

Attachment protest vs. a boundary

Stepping back can be a strategy, a limit, a punishment, or goodbye.

Sleep, grief, caregiving, substances

Start here. A brain on four hours of sleep looks like a different person.

Sometimes it is not psychology

Incentives, values, and incompatibility are allowed explanations.
